RamanFitApp -- References for the baseline-correction methods
================================================================

Each baseline method in the "Preprocess" tab is a published algorithm.
The .m file implementing it is noted for each.


backcor  (backcor.m)
--------------------
Iterative asymmetric polynomial baseline estimation, minimizing a
non-quadratic cost function (symmetric/asymmetric Huber or truncated
quadratic).

  V. Mazet, C. Carteret, D. Brie, J. Idier, B. Humbert,
  "Background removal from spectra by designing and minimising a
  non-quadratic cost function",
  Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory Systems, 76(2), 121-133 (2005).

  V. Mazet, D. Brie, J. Idier,
  "Simultaneous spectrum estimation and baseline correction using
  sparse representations",
  Proceedings of EUSIPCO, pp. 305-308 (2004).

  V. Mazet, PhD Thesis, Universite Henri Poincare Nancy 1 (2005).

Implementation: V. Mazet (vincent.mazet@unistra.fr), public domain.


airPLS  (airPLS.m)
------------------
Adaptive Iteratively Reweighted Penalized Least Squares baseline
(a Whittaker smoother with asymmetric iterative reweighting).

  Z.-M. Zhang, S. Chen, Y.-Z. Liang,
  "Baseline correction using adaptive iteratively reweighted penalized
  least squares",
  Analyst, 135(5), 1138-1146 (2010).

Builds on:

  P. H. C. Eilers,
  "A perfect smoother",
  Analytical Chemistry, 75(14), 3631-3636 (2003).

  P. H. C. Eilers,
  "Baseline correction with asymmetric least squares smoothing"
  (unpublished report, University of Amsterdam, 2005).

  F. Gan, G. Ruan, J. Mo,
  "Baseline correction by improved iterative polynomial fitting with
  automatic threshold",
  Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory Systems, 82(1-2), 59-65 (2006).

Implementation: Zhimin Zhang, Central South University (2011), public
domain (MATLAB File Exchange).


SNIP  (snip.m)
--------------
Statistics-sensitive Non-linear Iterative Peak-clipping baseline
(clips each point to the average of its neighbors at growing distance;
optional log-log-sqrt transform for the "improved SNIP" variant).

  C. G. Ryan, E. Clayton, W. L. Griffin, S. H. Sie, D. R. Cousens,
  "SNIP, a statistics-sensitive background treatment for the
  quantitative analysis of PIXE spectra in geoscience applications",
  Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research B, 34, 396-402
  (1988).

Implementation: original, written for RamanFitApp from the published
algorithm description.


APLS  (apls.m)
--------------
Adaptive-weight Penalized Least Squares baseline: same
penalized-least-squares (Whittaker) fit as airPLS, but with a simpler,
statistically motivated weight -- the probability that each observed
value could have arisen, by chance, from Poisson-distributed background
noise alone, given the current baseline estimate.

  P. J. Cadusch, M. M. Hlaing, S. A. Wade, S. L. McArthur, P. R. Stoddart,
  "Improved methods for fluorescence background subtraction from Raman
  spectra",
  Journal of Raman Spectroscopy, 44(11), 1587-1595 (2013).
  DOI: 10.1002/jrs.4371

Implementation: original, written for RamanFitApp from the published
algorithm description (Poisson survival function computed via MATLAB's
built-in GAMMAINC, verified equivalent to the standard Poisson CDF, to
avoid a Statistics and Machine Learning Toolbox dependency).

Note: the original PDF of this paper is not included in this repository
(Wiley copyright) -- see the DOI above.
